**The short answer:** your site's layout isn't adapting to small screens. That's usually because something on the page has a fixed size, the theme or template is old, or a page builder's mobile settings were never adjusted. Small problems take minutes to fix, but a site that was never designed for phones is often cheaper to rebuild than to patch.

Most visitors to a typical business website arrive on a phone, and Google ranks the mobile version of your site. If it's broken on mobile, it's broken.

## Common symptoms and causes

| What you see | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| **Page scrolls sideways** | One element is wider than the screen, often an image, table or embed |
| **Tiny text, and you have to zoom** | The page is missing its mobile (viewport) setting, or is an old desktop-only design |
| **Overlapping text or images** | Elements were positioned by hand for desktop, common in drag-and-drop builders |
| **Menu doesn't open or is cut off** | A theme or script problem |
| **Buttons are hard to tap** | Buttons are too small or too close together |
| **Popups you can't close** | A desktop popup that doesn't fit the screen |
| **Very slow loading** | Oversized images or too many plugins |

## How to check

1. **Use a real phone,** on mobile data, not just a narrow desktop window.
2. **Tap through as a client would:** menu, a key page, the contact form.
3. **Run [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev)** to see speed and mobile problems.

## How to fix it

- **Page builders:** switch to the mobile view in the editor, as Wix, Squarespace and WordPress builders often store separate mobile settings.
- **Sideways scrolling:** find the element that's too wide and let it shrink to the screen.
- **Old themes:** update them on a test copy first, as an update can break other things.
- **Images:** resize and compress them, and serve smaller versions to phones.

## Patch or rebuild?

Patch it if the problems are limited to a few pages. Consider a rebuild if every page has issues, the design dates from before phones were the main way people browsed, or each fix breaks something else.

## Questions

### What does "responsive website" mean?

A responsive website automatically adjusts its layout to fit any screen, so the same site works on a phone, tablet and desktop without zooming or sideways scrolling. Every modern business website should be responsive.

### Does a bad mobile site hurt my Google ranking?

Yes, it can. Google mainly uses the mobile version of a site for indexing and ranking, and slow or awkward mobile pages also make visitors leave, so fixing mobile problems usually helps both search and enquiries.
