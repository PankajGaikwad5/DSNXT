**The short answer:** Instagram alone can be enough while you're testing an idea or selling mainly to people who already follow you. Once you want strangers to find you on Google or AI assistants, or you sell something that needs trust, you need a website too. Use Instagram to get attention and a website to turn that attention into clients.

## What Instagram does well

It's free, quick to start and very good at showing your work. For visual businesses like food, fashion, interiors and art, it's often where people first discover you.

## Where Instagram falls short

- **You don't own it.** Reach can drop overnight when the algorithm changes, and accounts can be hacked or suspended.
- **Search rarely leads there.** Someone asking Google or ChatGPT for a *"wedding photographer in Toronto"* is mostly shown websites and Maps listings.
- **It's hard to explain things.** Services, process, pricing guidance and past projects get buried in a feed.
- **It can look less established.** For bigger purchases, clients often look for a proper website before they make contact.
- **There's no real enquiry path.** DMs get lost, and you can't ask people the useful questions a form can.

## Instagram or website? A quick test

| Your situation | What you need |
|---|---|
| Testing an idea or a side project | Instagram is fine for now |
| Most customers come from followers and word of mouth | Instagram plus a simple one-page site |
| People search for what you offer | A website and a Google Business Profile |
| You sell high-value services or products | A proper website is essential |
| You sell products online regularly | A website or online store you control |

Referrals count too. Even when someone recommends you, the person they told will usually look you up before calling.

## What's the smallest website that works?

You don't need twenty pages to start. A single well-made page can do the job if it says what you do and where, shows your best work, includes some proof from past clients, and makes it easy to get in touch. You can add pages as the business grows.

Link it in your Instagram bio, so followers have somewhere to go, and set up a [Google Business Profile](/guides/get-business-on-google-maps) so local searchers can find you too.

## Questions

### Can a business survive without a website?

Yes, especially a small one that lives on referrals or a strong social following. But it depends on a platform it doesn't control, and it misses people searching on Google and AI assistants, so most growing businesses benefit from having one.

### Does having a website help my Instagram?

Yes. A website gives followers a place to learn more, enquire or buy, and lets you link to specific projects and products. It also gives search engines and AI tools something to read and cite.
