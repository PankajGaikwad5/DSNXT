**The short answer:** create a free **Google Business Profile** at [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business), add your details, and verify that you run the business. Once it's verified, your business can appear on Google Maps and in local search. You don't need a website to do this, and you never need to pay Google for it.

## How to set it up

1. **Check whether a listing already exists.** Search your business name on Google Maps. If it's there, choose the option to claim it instead of creating a duplicate.
2. **Sign in and add your business** using a Google account you'll keep. Use a business account, not one belonging to a former employee or agency.
3. **Use your real business name.** Stuffing in extra keywords breaks Google's rules and can get a listing suspended.
4. **Choose the most accurate primary category,** such as "Interior designer". This is one of the most important choices you'll make.
5. **Add your location or service area.** If clients don't visit you, you can hide your address and list the areas you serve.
6. **Verify.** Google will offer a method, which may be a video, phone, email or a postcard. Verification can take from minutes to a few weeks.

## How to show up higher

- **Fill in everything:** hours, services, description, website and phone number.
- **Add real photos** of your work, your team and your premises.
- **Ask happy clients for reviews,** and reply to all of them.
- **Keep your name, address and phone number identical** on your profile, your website and directories.
- **Link to a good website.** Google uses it to understand what you do, and people check it before calling.

Google's map results mainly depend on relevance, distance and prominence. You can't change your distance from the person searching, so concentrate on the other two.

## Why isn't my business showing?

New profiles can take time to appear. Check that verification finished, then search your exact business name. If you appear for your name but not for *"interior designer near me"*, you're listed but not yet ranking, which is normal for a new listing. ## Questions

### Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a Google Business Profile is free. Be wary of callers who claim you must pay Google to appear on Maps or keep your listing.

### Can I be on Google Maps without a physical office?

Yes. If you visit clients or work online from home, you can set up as a service-area business, hide your address and list the areas you serve instead.
