**The short answer:** a WhatsApp button is a link to `https://wa.me/` followed by your full phone number, with the country code and no plus sign, spaces or dashes. Add that link to a button on your site, and tapping it opens a chat with you. It's free and doesn't need an app or a plugin.

## Step 1: Create your link

Write your number in international format, without `+`, `0`, spaces or brackets:

> https://wa.me/15551234567

Test it on your phone before adding it to the site. A wrong format is the most common reason these links fail.

## Step 2: Add a pre-filled message (optional)

You can start the conversation for your visitor by adding text to the link, with spaces written as `%20`:

> https://wa.me/15551234567?text=Hi%2C%20I%27d%20like%20to%20ask%20about%20your%20services

Pre-filled text makes it easier to start, and tells you which page the person came from if you change the message per page.

## Step 3: Add it to your site

- **Any website:** add a button or a text link and paste in your `wa.me` link. This works in WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and custom sites.
- **A floating button:** many builders have a built-in option or a small app that keeps a WhatsApp icon in the corner of each page.
- **Custom sites:** ask your developer to add a lightweight link rather than a heavy third-party chat widget, which can slow your pages down.

## Where to put it

Place it where someone has just decided they're interested: at the end of a project or service page, in your contact section and in the menu on phones. A floating button works well on mobile as long as it doesn't cover other buttons or text.

For a business number, use **WhatsApp Business**. It's free and gives you a business profile, away messages and quick replies, so nobody gets silence outside working hours.

## Questions

### Is a WhatsApp button free?

Yes. A `wa.me` link is free to use, and the WhatsApp Business app is also free. You only pay if you use a paid chat widget or the WhatsApp Business Platform for large-scale messaging.

### Why is my WhatsApp link not working?

Usually the number is in the wrong format. It needs the country code and no plus sign, leading zero, spaces or dashes. Also check that the number is actually registered on WhatsApp.
